# 📊 Sales Performance Analysis | Data Analytics Case Study

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the sales performance of a retail product using a simulated daily sales dataset. The objective was to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), develop meaningful business KPIs, identify pricing and profitability trends, and present actionable insights through interactive dashboards and a business presentation.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from planning and data preparation to visualization and business storytelling.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Miro** – Flowchart and project planning
* **Canva** – Gantt Chart for project planning
* **Databricks** – SQL queries, data cleaning, feature engineering, and EDA
* **Microsoft Excel** – Interactive dashboard
* **Databricks Dashboards** – Business intelligence dashboard
* **Google Looker Studio** – Interactive dashboard
* **Power BI** – Business dashboard
* **Lovable** – Interactive web dashboard
* **Microsoft PowerPoint** – Executive presentation

---

## 📈 Key Metrics

* Total Sales
* Total Quantity Sold
* Average Unit Selling Price
* Unit Cost Price
* Gross Profit / Loss
* Gross Profit Margin (%)
* Gross Profit per Unit
* Running Profit
* Price Distribution
* Pricing vs Quantity Analysis

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Identified the transition from a loss-making period to sustained profitability.
* Compared unit selling price against unit cost to evaluate pricing performance.
* Analyzed daily sales, quantity sold, and gross profit trends over time.
* Investigated potential promotional periods using pricing and sales volume patterns.
* Evaluated profitability through gross profit and gross margin analysis.
* Delivered executive dashboards that support data-driven business decision-making.
